# SDG Mapping

- **SDG 4 — Quality Education:** supports hands-on learning about system resources, monitoring, visualization and data interpretation.
- **SDG 9 — Industry, Innovation and Infrastructure:** demonstrates software-based resource monitoring and evidence-based comparison.
- **SDG 11 — Sustainable Cities and Communities:** indirect contribution through awareness of responsible device use and digital sustainability.

These are qualitative contributions, not official certification or measured impact. CPU/RAM changes alone do not establish electricity savings.
